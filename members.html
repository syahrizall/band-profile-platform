Members

The four people in the room.

Arunika is a fixed lineup. These are the players on the records and on the dates.

Raka Pratama

Vocals, guitar

Writes most of the lyrics and sings them like a late radio note. He started the band after a run of open-mic nights in Dago.

Sinta Wijaya

Guitar

Arranges the turns that make a verse become a chorus. She keeps the harmony honest and the set in the right order.

Dimas Hartono

Bass

Joined in 2022 and holds the low end. The songs feel heavier live because of the lines he refuses to simplify.

Maya Kusuma

Drums

Drives the room without rushing it. She learned the kit in a school band and still counts the songs out loud in rehearsal.
